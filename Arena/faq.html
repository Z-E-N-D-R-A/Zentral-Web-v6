FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Zentral Arena • Help & Information

For any questions regarding Zentral accounts, please check the Chat FAQ.
Zentral Arena is an online gaming hub developed specifically for the Zendra community to play with anyone anywhere anytime. Zentral Chat has been integrated to allow players to chat while playing without switching tabs.
Yes, Zentral Arena is completely free. We believe free to play is the key to accessibility of Zentral Arena.
Zentral Arena is web-based and accessible on modern desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile browsers. Additional platforms may be introduced in the future.
If you encounter a bug or issue with Zentral Arena, please report it through the Feedback Form. We will look into the issue and update the server as soon as possible.
You can create a room in Zentral Arena by clicking the "create room" button on the top right corner. Then you can select the gamemode, room visibility, and the number of players.
To invite someone to your room, you can share the 6-digit room code or the invite link to the person. The code is found at the top of the room lobby and the link can be copied by clicking the chain icon on top right.
To join a room with a room code, paste the 6-digit code at the bottom right input bar and press enter.
Yes, you can leave the room even after the game has started if you are not the host. Hosts cannot leave the room until the game is either finished or all players have left.
You can toggle a built-in split screen of Zentral Chat by clicking the chat bubble icon on top right. This will allow you to play on the left and chat on the right.
Public rooms can be seen and joined by anyone with a Zentral account. Private rooms can only be joined by people with the room code.
