Common Inquiries

How can I obtain the membership?

Social Giveaways: Participate in recurring giveaway events hosted across all our official social channels.

Exclusive Events: Join our live streams or community events designed to open up new membership slots.

Contribution: Boost the Zentralverse by promoting our content or providing game-changing feedback and ideas.

Can I buy membership?

No. Membership cannot be purchased under any circumstance. It can only be earned through events or recognition.

How rare is membership?

Only a small percentage of Zentrals who demonstrate loyalty and activity earn this rank.

How do I increase my chances?

Stay active in the Zentral Chat, regularly play in Zentral Arena, and keep your Core status updated regularly.

What are the actual perks of being a Member?

Members gain access to the Premium Zentral Core Feed, the ability to equip custom rare badges, and exclusive access to private pre-released contents.

Is Membership permanent once earned?

Usually, yes. However, maintaining your status requires staying "synced" with the community. Extreme inactivity or protocol violations can lead to a status reset.

Do I need a Google-linked account to become a Member?

While not strictly required, we highly recommend linking your Google account to secure the account.
