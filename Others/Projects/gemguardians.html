Story of Gem Guardians | Purple Gem Theory

Gem Guardians is a short movie recorded in Brawl Stars, telling the story of the purple gem, also known as Time Gem. It is a mesmerizing purple glowing hexagonal shape object that was first discovered in 1948. Besides its beauty, legends and beliefs surrounding the purple gem suggest several possibilities for its power like connection to the spirit realm and control the timeline of our universe. It is thought to hold a link to a realm beyond our own, where mysteries are waiting to be discovered by the main characters, Fang and Maisie.
