Cash, Shop & Progression Mechanics
  • Daily Quests: Implemented a 24-hour resetting tier layout: Easy (Rewards $100 Cash), Medium (Rewards $250 Cash), and Hard (Rewards $500 Cash).
  • Shop System: 7 new utility assets: Pizza ($100), Burger ($100), Fireworks ($250), Hammer ($500), SpeedCoil ($750), GravityCoil ($750), and Glider ($1000).
  • Warehouse Leaderboards: Added a competitive network-wide Cash Leaderboard visualization matrix inside the central warehouse node.
Coin and Redeem Codes
  • Coin System: New Coin system is here! There will be 37 coins scattered around the map, picking each of them up will give you 250 Cash. Apart from that, there will also be 3 purple color special hidden coins that will give you 1000 Cash.
  • Redeem Codes: 10 new secret codes in the game that can give you 100-1000 coins. These codes will be hidden across all ZendraCrazy social platforms!
Zendra Railway Network Overhaul

Implemented 4 high-velocity transit networks syncing city districts:

  • Zendraland Line: Runs localized routing between Zendraland and the Airport.
  • Sakumo Line: Establishes transit linkage between City of Zendra and Island of Sakumo.
  • Walmart Line: Direct transit connection linking Zendraland and Island of Walmartbag.
  • Bunny Line: Establishes rapid transit between City of Zendra and Island of Bunny.
