PROJECT ZEN

Origin of Zen

Zen is a powerful artificial intelligence designed to serve as an adaptable digital assistant. Equipped with advanced multi-tier features and unparalleled ecosystem flexibility, Zen aimed to revolutionize how users navigate daily scheduling tasks and complex programmatic challenges.

Whether managing complex priorities, orchestrating community channels, or engaging in meaningful situational dialogue, Zen was engineered to seamlessly integrate into your workflow, providing robust performance and reliable companionship every step of the way.

Economy Mechanics Restructuring

  • Box Containment Rework: Implemented Zen Box (drops 2,000–3,500 Zeni + 3 Common/Rare/Epic items) and Ultra Box (drops 8,500–10,000 Zeni + guaranteed Ultra/Zodiac tier item). Obtainable via performance quests, milestones, events, or via /economy open.
  • Crafting & Fusion Pipelines: Users can synthesize assets into high-tier Fusion items via /economy craft. Standard variants require 3 material items + 1 Fusion Token; Mega Fusion options require 5 items + 1 Mega Fusion Token. View structural item paths via /economy item-info.
  • Badges & Profile Titles: Earn badges by unlocking rarity collection tiers or achieving milestone goals. Badges generate periodic passive Zeni payments, fully replacing legacy role income structures (claimable via /economy collect or managed inside the market via /economy buy).
  • Milestone Progression Tracking: Added progress tracking metrics driven purely by total Zeni earnings. Advancing milestone layers unlocks specialized tools, badge assets, and dynamic currency bundles without losing progression due to economy losses. Check via /economy milestone.
  • Item Class Adjustments: Added Ultra, Zodiac (time-locked to specific calendar cycles), and Fusion categories. Unlocking items populates user tracking logs accessible via /economy collections.
  • Quests & Cooperative Events: Implemented 5 specialized quest frameworks containing 5 progressive performance stages checked via /economy quests. Added 4 global Community Events rotating on a 2-week cycle tracked through /economy event.
  • Store Management Update: Store profiles display rotating daily freebies and custom bundle drops resetting every 24 hours. Purchase or claim using product keys via /economy buy.
