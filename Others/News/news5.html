Zentral-Web v6.2 Update - Play with your friends like NEVER BEFORE at Arena!!!
23 June 2026
We are thrilled to announce a whole list of updates and improvements coming to Zentral-Web in version 6.2, especially Zentral Arena, a place for you to interact and play different games with your besties!
Zentral Arena
Zentral Chat opened the window for chatting globally, Zentral Core opened the window for expressing freely, and now, Zentral Arena is opening a window for playing anytime, anywhere, with anyone!
These are the games available in Zentral Arena:
  • Mahjong
  • SHU
  • Big 2
  • Blackjack
  • Poker
You can create a private room and share the room code to invite your friends to play together. You can also create a public room to match with other online players.

To make it easier to communicate with others while playing, we have integrated Zentral Chat into the Arena for you to message your friends while playing without switching between apps. Open the chat by simply pressing the chat icon on top right.
We believe Zentral Arena will help you further connect with your friends. Try out Zentral Arena now at here!
If you have any inquiries with Zentral Arena, feel free to check out Arena FAQ.
Silly Dodges Improvements
We have listened to feedbacks from our community and improved the overall experience.
  • Increased Silly Particles spawn rate (33% faster)
  • Decreased Combo Boost from Silly Particles (35 to 30)
  • Constant increase in difficulty (500 frames to 100 frames)
  • Improved SFX sound quality and volume
  • Fixed inconsistent bullet speeds
Other Changes and Fixes

  • Fixed Brawl Commands not working in Zentral Chat
  • Completed Project Pages - Zen, Gem Guardians, and City of Dreams
  • Improved Terminal Page with new prompts and commands
  • Updated Zentral Membership Page UI
  • Updated Milestones for Others Page
