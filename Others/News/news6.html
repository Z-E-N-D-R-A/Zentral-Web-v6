Closing a Beautiful Chapter — A Farewell from Zendra
15 July 2026
After three and a half amazing years of content creation, the time has come for me to say goodbye to this chapter of my life.
It's time, Goodbye
Throughout this journey, I’ve had the privilege of meeting incredible friends from all over the world and working on so many precious projects. These are memories I will treasure and carry forward with me for the rest of my life. On behalf of the entire KuraWorks team, I want to say the deepest thank you to every single Zentral who made all of this possible.
When I first created this channel back in 2023, I was lost and completely unsure of what the future held. I never could have imagined that a small idea would grow to reach over 7.7 million views. Over the last three and a half years, I have grown and learned so much, and it was my Zentrals who gave me the purpose to keep going. Honestly, after all this time, it’s hard to imagine a life without this channel and without all of you.

The End marks a New Beginning
As one last token of our appreciation, we want to make sure the digital home we built together stays open for you. All current website users will be granted free lifetime access to their memberships. The KuraWorks team and I wanted to ensure you can continue to enjoy the space we all created without any barriers.
Thank you, Zentral, for three and a half years of pure magic, laughter, and unforgettable milestones. Leaving this channel behind is one of the hardest things I’ve ever had to do, but I step away knowing we built something truly special. Take care of yourselves, support each other, and let’s make this final month an absolute celebration.
