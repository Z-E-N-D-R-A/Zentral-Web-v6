New Channel Update, Removal of Design Store, Massive Update to Zen and MORE!
23 June 2024
This month, we are announcing some exciting massive reworks and changes to both our channel and our Discord bot, Zen, as well as new projects coming very soon and more! We have been conducting surveys and collecting data for months to improve this community, and here are the changes coming...
Channel and Social Update
To start off, our channel will be getting new intros and outros! Since summer is here, there will also be a new upload schedule.
  • July & August: Videos every Saturday
  • First week: extra upload on Wednesday
  • Second week: YouTube Shorts on Tuesday & Thursday
  • Weeks rotate
Unfortunately, I will not be able to livestream this summer due to vacation, but streaming will resume on YouTube and Twitch after August.
We will also be more active on Discord, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter (X), and Twitch, aiming to expand this community and deliver high-quality content.
Design Store and Zen
Design Store has been removed and designs can no longer be ordered through the website. However, you can still receive free designs through our Discord bot, Zen.
Introducing the new Zen Economy Store. Earn Zeni using commands like /work and /crime, then purchase logos, banners, wallpapers, roles, and more.
Zen also features collectible items via /hunt and /loot. Rare items like the Legendary Phoenix can be sold for 10,000 Zeni or kept in your inventory.
NEW SYSTEMS INTRODUCED:
  • 🎟 Ticket System (/ticket)
  • 🎁 Giveaway System (/giveaway)
We’ve also reworked moderation, added new warning commands, and introduced over 35 new commands. More systems such as tournaments and auto-mod are planned.
Future Plans
Zen is only one of many mega projects currently in development. Thank you to all Zendra Team members and community supporters.
More updates will be posted here — stay tuned.
