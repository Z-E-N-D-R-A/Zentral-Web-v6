How to Play

Controls & Phase Dash

Steer with mouse, touch drag, or WASD/Arrow keys. Press Space / Shift (or tap DASH) for an invulnerable burst through tight gaps.

3-Shield Forgiveness

You start with 3 shields. Taking a hit triggers a shockwave that clears nearby bullets and grants brief invulnerability.

Classic & Survival

Classic: Collect green energy orbs and graze bullets to stack combos. Survival: Outlast escalating bullet waves as long as possible.

Power-Up Orbs

Cyan (S): +1 Shield. Violet (T): Chrono Slow-Mo. Gold (M / E): Orb Magnet in Classic, or Screen-Clear EMP Blast in Survival! Pink (D): Rapid Dash in Survival.
